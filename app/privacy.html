The small print

Privacy

The short version: your details stay in your browser. Last updated 15 September 2026.

What we collect

Nothing about you, on our side. There are no accounts and no sign-up. The mortgage details you type in, and any email address you enter for a reminder, are saved only in your own browser's local storage so they're still there next time. They are not sent to us. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Cookies and tracking

We don't set cookies and we don't run analytics or advertising trackers. Local storage is used only to remember what you typed.

Who else sees anything

  • Hosting. The site is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log the IP address and browser details of visitors to run the service. Their privacy statement applies.
  • Market data. Your browser fetches live rate data directly from our data provider. That provider sees the request, including your IP address, but nothing you typed.
  • Fonts. The page loads a typeface from Google Fonts, which sees the request in the same way.

Reminders

If you ask for a reminder, at the moment it is saved in your browser only and nothing is sent. If we add real email reminders, we'll ask for your consent at that point and update this notice.

Your rights

Because we don't hold personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you think that's wrong, or have any question about privacy on this site, please get in touch and we'll answer.

Changes

If what we collect changes, this page will say so, with a new date at the top.
